DPODPCO Cookie Consent SDK

This demonstration shows how client corporate websites integrate the ultra-lightweight Vanilla JS cookie consent banner. It automatically records consent logs to the DPODPCO platform for NDPC regulatory audit defense.

1. How to Embed on Client Websites

Simply paste this snippet before the closing </body> tag:

<script 
  src="http://127.0.0.1:8000/embeds/dp-consent.js" 
  data-tenant-id="tenant-uuid-12345" 
  data-company-name="First National Fintech Ltd"
  data-primary-color="#059669"
  data-privacy-url="/privacy-policy">
</script>
