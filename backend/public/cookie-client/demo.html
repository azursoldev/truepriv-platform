TruePriv Zero-Dependency Cookie Consent SDK

This demonstration shows how client corporate websites integrate the ultra-lightweight Vanilla JS cookie consent banner. It automatically records proof-of-consent logs to the DPODPCO platform for NDPC regulatory audit defense and gates third-party scripts.

1. TruePriv Script Tag Blocking (Zero GTM Required)

Notice how third-party scripts (e.g., Google Analytics, Meta Pixel) are marked with type="text/plain" data-dp-category="analytics". The browser will NOT execute them until the visitor accepts the Analytics category!

⏳ Analytics Script Status: BLOCKED (Awaiting visitor consent for "Analytics")
⏳ Marketing/Ad Pixel Status: BLOCKED (Awaiting visitor consent for "Marketing")

2. How to Embed on Client Websites

Simply paste this snippet before the closing </body> tag:

<script 
  src="http://127.0.0.1:8000/embeds/dp-consent.js" 
  data-tenant-id="tenant-demo-fintech" 
  data-company-name="First Atlantic Fintech Nigeria Ltd"
  data-primary-color="#059669"
  data-position="bottom_bar"
  data-privacy-url="/privacy-policy">
</script>
