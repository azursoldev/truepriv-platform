Data Protection Training

Statutory Workforce Capacity Building & Masterclasses

Transform human vulnerability into your strongest privacy perimeter

Under Section 33 and 34 of the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023, data controllers and processors are statutorily required to ensure that all personnel who handle personal data receive adequate, continuous training. During annual CAR filings, evidence of staff competence is an explicit regulatory scoring requirement.

Amstel Consulting delivers CPD-accredited executive retreats, certified DPO masterclasses, enterprise-wide e-learning modules, and practical data protection compliance drills designed for high-retention adult learning.

  • C-Suite & Boardroom Data Protection Governance Masterclasses
  • Intensive Certified Data Protection Officer (DPO) Bootcamps
  • Enterprise NDPA 2023 Staff Privacy E-Learning & SCORM Modules

STAGE 01 • FOUNDATION

Annual Compliance Check
