Fintech & Digital Payments

Accelerating high-velocity transactions, Open Banking APIs, and automated underwriting while maintaining ironclad privacy engineering and statutory NDPA alignment.

High-velocity payments meets statutory privacy governance

Nigeria’s fintech ecosystem powers millions of daily micro-transactions, digital wallet top-ups, and cross-border remittances. With velocity comes vulnerability: mobile device telemetry, location scraping, and transactional metadata require rigorous lawful basis justification under NDPA Section 24.

We engineer compliance into payment gateways, neobank apps, and POS switch networks, transforming privacy by design from an engineering hurdle into an enterprise market differentiator that facilitates swift license renewals with CBN and NDPC.

Automated Profiling DPIA & Third-Party API Sandboxing

Under NDPA Section 37, fintechs employing machine learning algorithms for instant credit scoring or fraud scoring must afford data subjects the right not to be subject to solely automated decisions having legal effects without human recourse.

Amstel Consulting conducts end-to-end algorithmic DPIAs and sandbox assessments for Open Banking API consumers, validating consent capture strings and token expiration policies.

Explore Fintech Compliance Solutions

Cross-border remittances & Section 41 Data Flows

Fintechs routing payments through international infrastructure (Stripe, AWS, Cloudflare, Plaid) face strict extraterritorial transfer obligations. Section 41 mandates verified adequacy or approved standard contractual clauses.

Our team structures customized International Data Transfer Agreements (IDTAs) and conducts transfer impact assessments that protect African fintech unicorns from cross-border regulatory sanctions.

Open Banking API Token Hygiene & Consent Scopes

Auditing OAuth2 scopes, webhook data hygiene, and end-user consent revocation mechanisms across mobile payment applications.

Fast-Track DCMI Regulatory Classification

Advising fast-growing payment switches on statutory Data Controller of Major Importance registration thresholds and annual CAR audits.
