Elevate privacy into board-level fiduciary assurance and investor-grade ESG trust

Data protection is no longer an isolated IT operational topic; it is an inescapable component of corporate governance. Under Principle 14 of the Nigerian Code of Corporate Governance (NCCG) 2018 and the NDPA 2023, corporate directors bear personal fiduciary responsibility for systemic risk oversight, data security failures, and consumer rights violations.

Amstel Consulting advises Boards of Directors, Audit Committees, and Institutional Investors across West Africa. We structure board risk charters, craft ESG digital trust disclosures, execute pre-acquisition M&A privacy due diligence, and insulate executive leadership from statutory liability.
