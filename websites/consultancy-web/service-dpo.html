Outsourced DPO (DPO-as-a-Service)

Statutory Section 32 & Executive Privacy Oversight

Access licensed DPCO executive privacy leadership without full-time overhead

Under Section 32 of the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023, data controllers of major importance (DCMIs) must designate a dedicated, qualified Data Protection Officer (DPO). The law explicitly permits retaining an external expert organization to perform this statutory role.

Amstel Consulting’s Outsourced DPO practice provides certified privacy executives, dedicated 30-day Data Subject Access Request (DSAR) desks, and a rapid 72-hour regulatory breach response team, delivering board-level governance at a fraction of in-house recruitment costs.

  • Formal registration of certified DPCO partner with NDPC as your official DPO
  • End-to-end 30-day statutory citizen DSAR request management desk
  • 24/7 incident triage with 72-hour Section 40 breach escalation command
