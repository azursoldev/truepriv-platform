AI Consulting & Governance

Responsible, Explainable & Auditable Artificial Intelligence

Deploy transformative machine learning while neutralizing algorithmic regulatory liability

As Nigerian enterprises deploy predictive analytics, large language models (LLMs), and automated underwriting engines, artificial intelligence brings unprecedented legal accountability. Under Section 37 of the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023, data subjects possess the fundamental right not to be subjected to decisions based solely on automated processing that produce significant legal effects.

Amstel Consulting bridges state-of-the-art machine learning engineering with licensed DPCO legal oversight. We audit inference pipelines, eliminate algorithmic bias, design human-in-the-loop fallback architectures, and establish board-level AI governance frameworks aligned with ISO 42001.

  • Algorithmic bias, explainability (XAI), and fairness audits
  • Statutory Section 37 automated profiling defensibility reviews
  • Generative AI training data privacy and prompt telemetry governance
