Compliance Audit Return (CAR)

Official DPCO Statutory Filing & Assurance

Ensure Bulletproof Compliance. Avoid Statutory Penalties. File Your Annual CAR with Absolute Confidence.

Under Section 33 of the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023, data controllers and processors of major importance must undergo an independent data protection audit and file their Compliance Audit Return (CAR) annually with the NDPC. Failing to file, or submitting an inaccurate audit, exposes your organization to severe regulatory fines of up to ₦10,000,000, personal director liability, and critical reputational damage.

As your licensed DPCO partner, we eliminate the operational burden of filing your CAR. We handle the entire lifecycle seamlessly: Comprehensive Data Discovery, Gap Identification, and Immutable Documentation & Submission to the NDPC.
