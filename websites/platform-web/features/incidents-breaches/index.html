The Problem

Privacy compliance should not depend on scattered spreadsheets and memory.

Requirements change, but information often sits in email, documents and disconnected trackers. Teams lose time collecting the same facts, finding the right owner and reconstructing proof of decisions.

Less manual drafting

Automate approximately 90–95% of repetitive document creation, RoPA records, and assessments from structured answers instead of starting from a blank page.

Clear responsibilities

Give departments unambiguous tasks, automated reminders, and provide management with a current view of risks, deadlines and progress.

Evidence you can retrieve

Preserve tamper-evident audit trails, versioning, review notes, and management approvals without reconstructing compliance history during audits.

Operating Engine

From answers to reviewed compliance records.

A repeatable four-step process that turns complex regulatory mandates into everyday operational tasks.

1

Ask plain-language questions

Ask the right people plain-language questions about a processing activity across business units.

2

Reuse approved information

Reuse approved information about systems, vendors, data classifications and security controls.

3

Generate records & DPIAs

Generate a RoPA entry and, where screening identifies a trigger, a draft DPIA with gaps and proposed safeguards.

4

Review, approve & preserve

The DPO or assigned reviewer checks the draft; management approves accountable decisions; the platform tracks actions and preserves evidence.

OPERATING SYSTEM

Keep privacy work moving from obligation to evidence.

TruePriv connects the people, information and decisions behind a privacy programme. Business owners answer guided questions, the platform prepares structured records and assessments, and privacy professionals review material conclusions before they are approved. Set up your organisation and applicable country pack. Invite the people who know each process. Reuse approved information about systems, vendors and controls. Review generated records, address gaps, approve decisions and export an auditable history.

Automation supports consistent work; it does not replace professional judgement or management accountability.

SOLUTIONS

Make privacy compliance part of everyday operations.

Give your teams a clear way to describe their processing, complete assigned actions and raise concerns. Privacy leads can review what has changed, and management can see unresolved work without losing the evidence behind each decision.

Start with an organisational profile, invite owners, build a processing inventory and identify gaps. Use guided assessments to decide which changes require deeper review. Assign actions, monitor deadlines and prepare reports from approved records.

SOLUTIONS

Run the work behind credible privacy advice.

Internal DPOs: Keep an annual work plan, monitor obligations, document advice and escalate matters with a traceable history. Outsourced DPOs: Work within the client's authorised workspace while preserving reporting lines, confidentiality and a record of recommendations.

Consultants and DPCOs

Manage delegated work across separate client organisations. A portfolio view can help prioritise deadlines and deliverables, while client records, permissions and audit histories remain separate. Engagements that involve independent auditing and operational DPO support require appropriate separation and conflict checks.

The platform supports qualified professionals; using it does not confer a DPO appointment, professional licence or regulatory accreditation.
